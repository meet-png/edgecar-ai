Battery passport check

Checks the vehicle's signature on a battery report, entirely in this browser: nothing you paste or scan is sent anywhere, and the report itself travels after the # in the link, which browsers never send to a server.

What a valid signature proves: the figures were produced by the software of the vehicle holding this key, from its own recorded data, and have not been changed since. Compare the key ID with the one the vehicle shows (or, in production, with the manufacturer's certificate for that vehicle). It does not prove the sensors were honest — that is the job of the vehicle's secure boot and hardware key store.
